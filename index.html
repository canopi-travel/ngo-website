Connecting Communities to Nature, Digitally

Canopi Nature Society (CNS) empowers NGOs and indigenous communities to grow their digital reach and drive collaborative impact for nature restoration and conservation.

Our Mission

Canopi Nature Society is a non-profit society registered in Canada. Our mission is to help NGOs and indigenous communities increase their digital reach through collaborative nature restoration efforts.

We operate the canopi.travel platform — a regenerative travel and nature adoption marketplace that connects conscious travellers with vetted conservation partners around the world.

🌊

Regenerative Travel

Experiences that fund NGO work on the ground — from coral reef restoration dives to forest and wildlife missions.

🌿

Adopt Nature

Adopt a piece of coral or a tree and follow its growth through real field monitoring, wherever you are in the world.

✦

Future Digital Experiences

New digital experiences in development that will drive awareness and donations to restoration and conservation causes.
